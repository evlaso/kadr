    Glossary

    Term Meaning
    Capture bar The bar that chooses a screenshot, text recognition or recording mode.
    Area; picker (overlay) An area is the rectangle to capture; the picker is the overlay where you select and adjust it.
    Thumbnail (card) The small preview after a capture, with actions such as Copy and Edit.
    Pin on top Keep a screenshot floating above other windows.
    Editor The window for drawing on a screenshot and setting its background.
    Canvas The editor's work area, where the picture and shapes are shown.
    Tool A way to select, draw or change something in the editor, such as Arrow or Text.
    Preset A saved look you can apply again in the editor or studio.
    Background The backdrop around a picture or video, with padding, corners and shadow.
    Hide Cover part of a picture with pixelation, blur or a black box.
    Studio; Studio mode Studio edits recordings; Studio mode records the cursor and camera separately so their look can be changed there.
    Zoom A studio interval that brings part of the recording closer.
    Cut A piece left out of the studio's exported video.
    Trim Shorten a recording at its beginning or end.
    Filmstrip The row of video frames on the studio timeline; its yellow edges trim the recording.
    Camera bubble The camera preview placed over the recording, with an adjustable size and shape.
    Project (.kadr) A file that keeps the source media and the whole edit for reopening.
    Tray icon The desktop icon that opens settings and has a capture menu; it also holds global hotkeys on macOS and Windows.
    History The searchable list of screenshots and recordings.
    Shortcut (hotkey) A key combination for an action; global shortcuts work outside kadr's windows too.
