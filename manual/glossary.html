kadr manual

Glossary

TermMeaning
Capture barThe bar that chooses a screenshot, text recognition or recording mode.
Area; picker (overlay)An area is the rectangle to capture; the picker is the overlay where you select and adjust it.
Thumbnail (card)The small preview after a capture, with actions such as Copy and Edit.
Pin on topKeep a screenshot floating above other windows.
EditorThe window for drawing on a screenshot and setting its background.
CanvasThe editor's work area, where the picture and shapes are shown.
ToolA way to select, draw or change something in the editor, such as Arrow or Text.
PresetA saved look you can apply again in the editor or studio.
BackgroundThe backdrop around a picture or video, with padding, corners and shadow.
HideCover part of a picture with pixelation, blur or a black box.
Studio; Studio modeStudio edits recordings; Studio mode records the cursor and camera separately so their look can be changed there.
ZoomA studio interval that brings part of the recording closer.
CutA piece left out of the studio's exported video.
TrimShorten a recording at its beginning or end.
FilmstripThe row of video frames on the studio timeline; its yellow edges trim the recording.
Camera bubbleThe camera preview placed over the recording, with an adjustable size and shape.
Project (.kadr)A file that keeps the source media and the whole edit for reopening.
Tray iconThe desktop icon that opens settings and has a capture menu; it also holds global hotkeys on macOS and Windows.
HistoryThe searchable list of screenshots and recordings.
Shortcut (hotkey)A key combination for an action; global shortcuts work outside kadr's windows too.
